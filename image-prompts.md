## General Image Rules

- Prefer real photography over illustrations for credibility.
- Keep faces, uniforms, and tools realistic.
- Avoid readable brand names unless the asset is custom-made for Yes We Clean.
- Avoid cluttered homes, dirty rooms, extreme damage, unsafe roof work, or fear-based imagery.
- Leave enough crop room for responsive layouts.
- Use consistent warm daylight and clean residential environments.
- Save service-card images at roughly `1600x1000`.
- Save owner portrait at roughly `1200x1500`.
- Compress final images before publishing.
